Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Thank you for using SpanLex - Spanish Dictionary (“SpanLex,” “the App,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

SpanLex does not require you to create an account or log in to use its core dictionary features. We do not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or account password.

Words you search for, saved or favourite words, selected language preferences, and other preferences may be stored locally on your device. The App itself does not send those local dictionary searches or saved words to our own servers.

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the details of your request. We use that information to respond to you and handle your inquiry.

Technical and Diagnostic Information

Depending on the services enabled in the App and on your device, third-party services may automatically process limited technical information, which may include:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and general app diagnostics
  • Crash reports and performance information
  • Language or regional settings
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising-related signals, where applicable

This information may be used by the relevant service providers to deliver their services, troubleshoot problems, measure performance, and help maintain app stability. The information collected depends on which services are active in your version of the App.

2. Dictionary Content and Local Storage

SpanLex provides Spanish vocabulary and dictionary content, which may include English translations, meanings, definitions, explanations, examples, related terms, and other language-learning information.

The dictionary database is packaged with or stored locally by the App. Looking up a word in that local database does not require the search query to be sent to our own servers.

Recent searches, bookmarks or favourite words, language preferences, and other app settings may be saved on your device. This locally stored information is not uploaded to or stored on our servers by SpanLex.

Local information may be removed if you delete it within the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App, subject to your device's backup and operating-system settings.

3. Text-to-Speech and Pronunciation

SpanLex may use the text-to-speech functionality available on your device to pronounce Spanish words or other supported text. Availability and pronunciation quality depend on your device, installed voice data, and system settings.

The App does not record your voice through this feature. If your operating system or selected speech service processes text externally, that processing is governed by the applicable provider's terms and privacy policy.

Advertising providers may automatically collect or receive information such as:

Providers may use this information to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize ads, subject to applicable law and your consent choices. Where required, the App or its providers may present consent options for advertising-related processing.

For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy and the Google information about advertising.
